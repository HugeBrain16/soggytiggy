Bands & artists I like (in no particular order): - None - Infant Annihilator / Nekrogoblikon - Children of Bodom - Death - Blood Incantation - Hermóðr - Dråpsnatt - Skogen - Avenged Sevenfold - Car Seat Headrest - Underworld - Butthole Surfers - Stone Temple Pilots - Juice Leskinen - Leevi and the Leavings - Lana Del Rey - Jamiroquai - Boards of Canada - Wan Bushi - Lapfox Trax - Basshunter - Polaventris - Darkthrone - Bathory - Burzum

These are albums that are special to me. I listen to way more music, especially death/black metal, but I can't really name many albums since theres so many different artists. I'll update this whenever I find something new and wonderful that I enjoy. Also there are many cases where I enjoy many songs from different albums from the same artist and I can't pick a favorite (e.g Children of Bodom).

To Be Alone

Hermóðr

This was the album that got me into atmospheric bm, the artist has such a refined style. The album has stuck with me for couple years now.

The Elysian Grandeval Galeriarch

Infant Annihilator

The first time I heard anything from infant annihilator, I wondered how anyone could listen to such noise. Now that I have finally learned to listen, I'm in love with the entire album. Anyone who knows the band knows why theyre so liked. The vocals, the instrumental, the unseriousness, the art.

Damp Chill of Life

None

This one is fairly new to me, only having discovered it this year. I've been trying to get more into DSBM and this album has stuck out. I love the sample in "you did a good thing". The melodies are very nice and feels very cold.

Ley

Approaching Mountains

This one feels nostalgic, its my most streamed purely because its my sleeping album... not in a bad way of course. I like how every song stands out, sounding different but fitting. The album cover is also very special to me.

Imaginal Disk

Magdalena Bay

This one is also new, only discovered this year. Wonderful sounds, ideas and effects. Favs are image, vampire in the corner and tunnel vision.

Avenged Sevenfold

Avenged Sevenfold

Summer of 2023 I started to get really into A7X. I dont think I listened to anything else during that time. The band was also something me and my now partner bonded over. One of the first "favorite" albums I ever discovered myself.

Life Is But A Dream...

Avenged Sevenfold

This was released in the peak of my A7X listening era. This lead to it being my most streamed album. My least fav track is mattel, only to say how much I love the others.

Brat

Charli XCX

Summer of 2024. Its my first album I listened fully on my own. I would have it on loop while playing stalker anomaly. This made it so it reminds me of stalker every time. My least favorite song is apple. My favorite nowadays is I think about it all the time. I think its a really deep and thought-provoking song.

Born To Die

Lana Del Rey

My friend who has a car owns this cd, and since its her driving she will choose the music. Ive listened to this so many times. Also listened to it a bunch while playing stalker. So it reminds me of late night driving and stalker. One of the few albums where I like every song.

Geogaddi

Boards of Canada

Very good samples. Not much I can say about this one. It inspires me a lot.

Starspawn

Blood Incantation

I love the sound, its truly evil. It reminds me of Qu from all tomorrows. The vocals are amazing, the guitar is amazing, I love it.

Blood Fire Death

Bathory

Its hard for me to say a fav album from bathory (name is based on it btw :D), since I mostly just listen to random songs from them. This one has a good sound to it from the couple songs that I have heard. Ill listen to it once I can really listen. The vocals sound like eric cartman to me, this is the main reason I found it slightly hard to get into it.

Monomania

Car Seat Headrest

Might be my favorite cshr album. So many memories in this one. It reminds me of my other friend who I used to "live" with. I guess we dont really talk that much now so it makes that album little more sad.

Black Goat of The Woods

Black Mountain Transmitter

Listened to this at 5am on winter nights while preparing for exams. I also was drinking a cold energy drink. What a good memory! The sound is really chilling.

Teens of Denial

Car Seat Headrest

Smoking album, thats what comes to mind everytime I listen to any songs from this. Oh also being very depressed. Cshr has this very different and cool way of describing stuff, I love it a lot. It also reminds me of the other friend. One good memory of this album was when I was sitting outside in the cold winter, smoking while looking at the green northern lights. I think about it a lot.

Hymner till undergången

Dråpsnatt

Another fairly new album for me. This album is something Ive never seen before. With the first time it keeps surprising the listener with everything they would want to hear. I would love to get a shirt from the band, too bad...
